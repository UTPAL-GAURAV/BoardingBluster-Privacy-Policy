# Privacy Policy - NordicLabs Boarding Bluster

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026

## Summary

This privacy policy outlines how NordicLabs collects, uses, and protects user information for the Boarding Bluster app.

- **Anonymous play** — no account required to play. A random player ID is generated on your device.
- **Optional sign-in** — Google Sign-In is only requested if you choose to unlock the Premium Fleet.
- **Leaderboard scores** — your best boarding times and an auto-generated display name are stored on our servers.
- **Crash reporting** — anonymous crash data is sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs.
- **No ads. No data sold.**

---

## Information We Collect

### Automatically generated (all users)
- A random anonymous player ID (UUID generated on your device — not linked to any account)
- An auto-generated display name (e.g. `Falcon847`) used on the leaderboard
- Your best boarding times per aircraft

### Only if you use Google Sign-In (optional, for Premium Fleet purchase)
- Your Google account ID (used solely to record and restore your purchase)
- We do not access your name, email, profile photo, or any other Google account data beyond the account ID

### Crash and error data
- Device type, OS version, app version, and stack traces are sent to Sentry for debugging
- No personally identifiable information is included in crash reports

---

## How We Use Your Information

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Anonymous player ID | Identify your scores on the leaderboard |
| Display name | Show your name on leaderboard rankings |
| Best times | Power the global leaderboard |
| Google account ID | Record and restore your Premium Fleet purchase |
| Crash data | Fix bugs and improve app stability |

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## Third-Party Services

Boarding Bluster uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

- **Supabase** — leaderboard and purchase storage (servers in EU: eu-west-1). [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Sentry** — crash reporting (EU-hosted). [sentry.io/privacy](https://sentry.io/privacy)
- **Google Sign-In** — optional authentication for purchases. [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

## Data Retention

- Anonymous scores and display names are retained indefinitely to maintain leaderboard history.
- Purchase records are retained to allow you to restore your Premium Fleet access after reinstallation.
- You may request deletion of your data by contacting us (see below).

---

## Children's Privacy

Boarding Bluster does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

---

## Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
